Privacy notice

Privacy notice

Fox Fix & Finish Property Maintenance processes the information you send through our website to review enquiries, prepare estimates, arrange appointments and respond to customers.

Information we collect

When you submit a request we collect your name, phone number, optional email address, postcode, preferred date and time, job description, linked product details and estimated basket information.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, provide a quotation, arrange the requested services and keep appropriate business records. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract and, where relevant, legitimate business interests or legal obligations.

Where information is handled

Your booking message opens in your selected messaging app, where you choose whether to send it. These messages are handled by your chosen messaging provider and by us after receipt. Product-link recognition may use Supabase and third-party services to process public retailer-page information, not your personal contact details. The website stores basket details in your browser to avoid losing your selections. External retailer product images may load from those retailers.

Retention and your rights

We keep customer details only as long as reasonably needed for your enquiry, providing services and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion of eligible information by contacting us at quotes@foxfixfinish.co.uk. You also have the right to raise concerns with the UK's Information Commissioner's Office.
